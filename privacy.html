Privacy

Short version: this site doesn’t collect anything about you.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no local storage. There’s no cookie banner because there’s nothing to agree to.

Analytics and other servers

There are no analytics, no tracking pixels, no embedded maps or videos and no fonts from other servers. Every file on the page, including the fonts, comes from this site. A Content Security Policy blocks requests anywhere else.

The booking form

Ursa is a concept project, so the booking form doesn’t send what you type anywhere. It checks your answers in your browser and shows a confirmation, and that’s all.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page and may keep them in its logs for security; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. I don’t have access to those logs.
